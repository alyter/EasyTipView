# Product Mission

> Last Updated: 2025-07-22
> Version: 1.0.0

## Pitch

PolyPal is a mobile iOS application that helps customers and vendors in the plastics industry discover and manage offers by providing seamless integration with Zoho Creator and advanced search capabilities with real-time chat functionality.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Plastics Industry Customers**: Buyers seeking specific plastic materials, components, or services
- **Plastics Industry Vendors**: Suppliers offering plastic materials, manufacturing services, or equipment

### User Personas

**Industry Buyer** (25-55 years old)
- **Role:** Procurement Manager, Operations Manager, or Business Owner
- **Context:** Needs to source plastic materials or services for manufacturing, construction, or retail operations
- **Pain Points:** Difficulty finding specific offers, lack of real-time communication with vendors, time-consuming search processes
- **Goals:** Find competitive offers quickly, communicate directly with suppliers, save preferred offers for future reference

**Plastics Vendor** (30-60 years old)
- **Role:** Sales Manager, Business Development, or Company Owner
- **Context:** Sells plastic materials, components, or manufacturing services
- **Pain Points:** Limited visibility for their offers, difficulty reaching target customers, managing customer inquiries efficiently
- **Goals:** Increase offer visibility, manage customer communications, create and update offers easily

**System Administrator** (25-45 years old)
- **Role:** IT Manager or Operations Manager
- **Context:** Manages the platform and user interactions
- **Pain Points:** Need to moderate conversations, manage user access, oversee offer quality
- **Goals:** Maintain platform quality, facilitate smooth user interactions, ensure security compliance

## The Problem

### Limited Offer Visibility and Discovery

Traditional methods for finding plastic industry offers are fragmented across multiple platforms and lack sophisticated search capabilities. This results in missed opportunities and inefficient procurement processes.

**Our Solution:** Advanced search functionality based on specific offer fields with real-time filtering and recommendations.

### Inefficient Communication Between Buyers and Suppliers

Current communication channels are often indirect, slow, and lack context about specific offers, leading to delays in negotiations and missed business opportunities.

**Our Solution:** Integrated chat functionality with customer and admin interfaces that maintains offer context throughout conversations.

### Lack of Personalized Experience

Existing platforms don't provide ways for users to save preferences, track favorite offers, or receive relevant notifications about new opportunities in their area of interest.

**Our Solution:** Personalized profiles with favorites, push notifications, and MFA-secured user accounts.

## Differentiators

### Zoho Creator Integration

Unlike generic marketplace apps, we provide seamless integration with Zoho Creator, ensuring data consistency and leveraging existing business processes. This results in real-time offer updates and reliable data synchronization.

### Industry-Specific Search

Unlike general B2B platforms, we provide search capabilities tailored specifically to the plastics industry with field-specific filters and terminology. This results in more accurate search results and faster discovery of relevant offers.

### Unified Mobile Experience

Unlike web-only platforms, we provide a native iOS mobile experience optimized for on-the-go professionals in the plastics industry. This results in better user engagement and accessibility for field workers and traveling professionals.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Advanced Offer Search:** Filter and search offers based on specific plastics industry fields and criteria
- **Detailed Offer View:** Comprehensive offer information with images, specifications, and vendor details
- **Secure Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for account security
- **User Profiles:** Personalized profile pages with preferences and account management

### Communication Features

- **Real-time Chat:** Direct messaging between customers and vendors with admin oversight
- **Admin Interface:** Administrative tools for managing conversations and user interactions
- **Push Notifications:** Real-time alerts for new offers, messages, and relevant updates

### Personalization Features

- **Offer Favorites:** Save and organize preferred offers for future reference
- **Offer Creation:** Vendors can create and manage new offers directly from the mobile app
